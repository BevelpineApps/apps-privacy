# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 31/08/2026

This privacy policy covers the Atlassian Marketplace apps **"Bulk Excel & CSV
Exporter for Jira"** and **"Hide from Export for Confluence"**.

## Summary

Neither app collects, stores, or transmits any personal data.

## What the apps do

- They run entirely within your Atlassian (Jira / Confluence) site.
- They read data from your site using **your own user permissions**.
- They never send data to any external server, and never store data.

## Personal data

- No personal data, account IDs, or user references are stored.
- No data is cached for any period of time.
- No analytics, tracking, cookies, or third-party services are used.
- No data ever leaves your Atlassian site.
